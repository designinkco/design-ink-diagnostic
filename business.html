How This Diagnostic Works

Skip ahead, take the diagnostic now →

Beautiful work doesn't automatically mean a profitable business.

The success of an interior design business comes down to two things: your systems and how you market yourself. This diagnostic looks at both, plus the pricing and positioning that sit underneath them.

It is not a quiz that ends with the same answer for everyone. There are four possible results, and one of them is "not yet".

The Three Pillars

Your score is built on the 3S Formula: Skills, Strategy, Systems.

Three questions on each, 30 points per pillar, shown as a total out of 100.

30 pts

Strategy

Your pricing, your positioning, and whether being busy turns into profit.

30 pts

Systems

How a project runs from first enquiry to final invoice, and how much of it lives in your head.

30 pts

Skills

The business skills, not the design ones: finding clients, holding your fee and handing work over.

Four Possible Results

You get one of these, with the reason why.

  1. Design for Success. More than one part of the business needs rebuilding, and you want someone beside you while you do it.
  2. Studio Strategy Session. Something is not working and you want a clear plan for what to fix first.
  3. AI Studio Setup. The business is sound, and the admin and documentation are what slow you down.
  4. Not yet. It is too early, or you do not have gaps I can help with. I will point you to free resources.

What You'll Get

In about four minutes, you will have:

  • Your score out of 100, with a bar for each pillar.
  • Your biggest gap, in one sentence.
  • One recommended next step, and what it involves.
  • A breakdown of the nine scored answers.
