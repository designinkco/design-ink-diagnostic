How This Diagnostic Works

Skip ahead, take the diagnostic now →

The studios losing work are not losing on talent. They are losing on the homepage.

When we benchmarked 18 of the most-respected interior design studios across the UK, Australia, and the US, the result surprised us. The studios with the most beautiful photography and the strongest brand recognition were not the ones with the most enquiries. Read the full research report →

The difference was not talent. It was how they used the homepage. The studios losing work were treating their website as a portfolio. The studios winning were treating it as a sales asset. This diagnostic measures which side of that line your site sits on, across the ten components proven to make the difference.

The Ten Components

Ten questions, each tied to a specific component.

Every winning site in the research does most of these. Every losing site does fewer than three.

  1. SEO title tag with service and location, not just your studio name.
  2. Above-the-fold CTA, repeated at least three times on the homepage.
  3. Tiered services with anchored pricing, even a "starting from" figure.
  4. Three named testimonials with photo or location attached.
  5. Press logo strip with publications you have been featured in.
  6. Awards or industry-body badges (SBID, BIID, AD Pro, equivalent).
  7. Founder photo and story surfaced on the homepage.
  8. Branded point of view in words, not just images.
  9. A journal or blog with at least quarterly posts.
  10. Trust-stacking footer with accreditations and social proof.
